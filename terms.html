AI Chat to Word is a Chrome browser extension that enables users to export conversations from AI chat platforms — including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, and Genspark — into Microsoft Word (.docx) and PDF (.pdf) formats.
